# Authors and research provenance

## Original master's research project

The project originated as an **M2P SCCI master's research project at Ensimag–UJF** and was developed by:

- **Abdourahmane Sakho**
- **Ali Mkhida**
- **Maad El Yadari**

The preserved original presentation is the canonical historical artifact for that collaboration.

## Current repository and ePrint track

The repository modernization, reproducibility layer, exact historical-math reconstruction, current ePrint draft, and the initial Rust port are currently maintained by **Ali Mkhida / Algorizk Labs**.

This does **not** unilaterally redefine authorship of the original research. Before an ePrint submission, the original collaborators should be contacted, shown the current draft, and asked whether and how they want to participate in the new paper.

## Authorship policy

The future paper author list will be based on confirmed contribution and consent. Historical credit remains explicit regardless of the final ePrint author list.
