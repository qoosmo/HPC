# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This is a research repository. Discussion should be technically rigorous,
professional, and respectful. Contributors are expected to:

- critique claims, proofs, experiments, and code rather than people;
- distinguish evidence from speculation;
- disclose uncertainty, failed experiments, and limitations relevant to a claim;
- respect authorship, provenance, licensing, and responsible-disclosure boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, or intimidation.

## Research disagreements

Strong disagreement is welcome when it improves the work. When challenging a
result, provide a reproducible counterexample, mathematical argument, failing
test, benchmark configuration, or precise source reference whenever possible.

## Historical material

Do not erase, rewrite, or misattribute the preserved historical project. Modern
corrections should be documented as corrections or new analysis rather than
silently substituted into the historical record.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate these
standards and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.
Concerns about repository conduct can be raised privately with the repository
owner through the GitHub account `@qoosmo`.
